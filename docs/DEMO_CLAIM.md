## The claim

A single body, born with reflexes and no training set, lived 139 simulated days in one room with one teacher. On day 139 it:

- came when called: six times on day 139 and eighteen on day 140 it walked to her at 0.2 m/s from 1.2 to 1.8 m and arrived (the
  lane's 'came', worth 2, paid only for its own way toward where she called from); day 138, the day before the born approach, once
  (C351, A216);
- looked at a named toy when asked: three times on day 140 ('the box? where is the box?' and its look on the box within her
  window: the lane's 'met_ask'), none on any day before (A217, A216 amended twice);
- named what it saw: 101 right names in the day (a toy's name said with that toy before its eyes: the lane's 'right_name'), 80
  the day before, 14 to 52 on days 130 to 134 (A202, the grounding organ);
- stood 73 times and stepped 160 times in the gait the room holds for it (W8), with no fall caught in the command blocks.

## What the child does itself (the brain's)

- The words it says: the voice's own choice, under dopamine from her face, with the grounding organ's prior on the word whose look
  fills its fovea (A202: the organ's rows are learned from its own hearings, nothing labelled).
- The command on the gait: the brain's locomotion effector (W8) steps the commanded speed and turn; its acts earn credit from her
  face as every act does. On day 139 its own acts were one held setting (the newborn's), and the walking came from the born road
  below (A216). The ruler over the days: the brain's own command aligning with her bearing, the cord's share falling.
- Its looks: the eyes' own acts, the born saccade to a cue (A186), and the heard word's cue (A202/A217).
- Its pain, its cry, its stress, its mood, its sleep's lessons (the nights: NREM's replay, REM's).

## What the room does for it (the environment's, disclosed)

- THE GAIT: in a command block the room holds Unitree's own pretrained locomotion policy at the legs at full strength (W7b, W8):
  the child does not balance or step; it commands. The gait is innate on this body as it is on a foal (W8's reading). The claim
  is never that it learned to walk.
- THE BORN APPROACH (A216): a cord step, below the gate, turns the body toward her face (and toward a heard word's look, and
  toward her voice's side at an onset) and walks it forward when facing her: the superior colliculus's crossed road (Dean,
  Redgrave and Westby 1989), a newborn's turn to its mother (Goren 1975; Muir and Field 1979), the attachment system's approach
  (Bowlby). Its sizes are ours. On day 139 this road did the coming; the brain's own command is measured against it from here.
- THE CATCH AND THE LAY: a fallen child is laid on its back and lifted again; the block's end lays it clear of the walls; a child
  wedged against a wall in pain is carried to its mat (C350, C352).
- THE FACE CUE'S STAND-IN (A157): the born face detector is read from the world (where her mouth is in its images), not from
  the pixels; its removal test is owed (A49's).
- THE ROOM'S WALLS: blocked ahead, the heading turns toward the room's middle; out of the rooms, the mat (W7b).

## What the teacher does (her method, the lead's)

- She calls it to come from 1.2 to 1.8 m, crouched (her face low enough for a body whose eye cameras look 47 degrees down), every
  200 ticks from her spot, and stays there until it comes (C351 and its amendments). Her lines are her own words (the birth
  words and the growth words she has entered); frames with other words were silent until 2026-10-10 (C351 amended twice).
- She names what it looks at, shows toys, confirms its acts, and smiles: the reward is her face (A1), its own pain the cost.
- In a command block she does not lean in, copy, attend or hand over (stay_put): it must come to her, not she to it.

## Not claimed

- Walking is not learned by the brain on this body (the fall-strength ruler stayed flat, W8). What is learned is the command.
- The coming on day 139 is the born road's, not the cortex's. Whether the cortex takes it over is the open ruler.
- The eyes' landing on a named thing is coarse (the periphery's fifteen cells), and her read of its look is the head's line,
  not the eyes' window (A216 amended twice): a gaze ask is met when the body faces the thing. met_ask was 2 on day 139.
- Generalisation beyond the one room is not shown (the owner's goal: a door onto a new room; the second room exists, D1).
- Nothing here is a benchmark. The numbers are one body's one life, read by its own rulers, with their chance levels
  (day 138 for the coming) named.
