# The brain systems map: the G1 design against an infant's first year

SD = docs/SIM_DESIGN.md:line, AR = ARCHITECTURE.md, IT = ITERATIONS.md; `wt_sim/` is the sim-core branch, `wt_world/` the world build. **P** present, **p** partial, **M** missing.

## The fact that governs everything below

Changing the body after birth makes a new body with a new seed (A20, SD:1835), and the design says a faster motor loop would be one (SD:1398). So every body mechanism here must be decided before birth (S6, SD:1334), or it costs the life. Seven of the gaps were already on the project's own "Body only" list (IT:774–789: items 20, 22, 26, 27, 29, 31 and 34) and never reached the design.

## The map

| system | ours | the infant, months 0–12 | the minimal faithful addition (source) |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1–V4/IT | **p**. The fovea's retina is the mean colour of 4-px cells (`wt_world/body/sim/eyes.py:63,98–102`). At 1.5 px/° (SD:273) that is about 0.19 c/°, with no edges. | Acuity rises from about 1 c/° at one month to about 12 c/° at twelve (Mayer et al. 1995). | A born centre-surround and oriented bank on the native pixels, as naive kittens have (Hubel and Wiesel 1963), with acuity rising by age (Vogelsang et al. 2018; contested, Katzhendler and Weinshall 2019). Sharper renders cost almost nothing (SD:1199). |
| auditory | **P** at the periphery: 40-band cochleas and ITD delay lines (SD:284, 1267). | Tuning narrows to the native phonemes at 6–12 months (Werker and Tees 1984). Words are segmented by statistics at 8 months (Saffran, Aslin and Newport 1996). | Nothing born. See development. |
| somatosensory | **p**. Force and onset per link; the joint sense (SD:287–288). | A body map forms. | Low priority: slip on the Dex3 links for grip force (Johansson and Westling 1984). |
| motor/premotor | **p**. Per-joint steps; `act_pred`, the forward half, `act_inv` (SD:297, 338–344). | Reaching at 3–5 months; its units merge over the year (von Hofsten 1991). | See the cerebellum. |
| association/PFC | **p**. 8 gated leaky bands (AR:181–201). The working-memory latch fires at utterance ends (AR:409–411). | Memory for a hidden object grows through 7–12 months (Diamond 1985). | Latch on R7's event ends (SD:1148) instead of on utterances. |
| hippocampus/EC | **p**. The frame key is unspecified (R7b, SD:1148); the cortex key had failed 0 of 30 (AR:284–287). Recall reaches only the words (`wt_sim/body/model.py:756`) and never a limb's proposal (`timing.py:306–315`). There is no heading or place code. | Deferred imitation after 24 h at 6–9 months (Meltzoff 1988; Barr, Dowden and Hayne 1996). | Key = the stream plus a heading integrated from the gyro (McNaughton et al. 2006). Value = the next frame plus the efference copy. Recall enters each effector's proposal through a map born at zero (Lengyel and Dayan 2007). |
| amygdala | **P**, specified, not built (SD:1000–1122). | Wariness follows locomotion, at 7–9 months (Campos et al. 1992). | None; `amyg_pav` off matches (SD:1077). |
| basal ganglia | **P**. One gate per limb, the three-factor lesson, the learned stop (SD:310–321; AR:480–533, 715–736). | Selection and habit. | Low priority: separate Go and NoGo weights (Collins and Frank 2014). |
| cerebellum | **M**. SIM_DESIGN never mentions it. The forward half runs inside the 150 ms tick (SD:340–341). | Grows 240% in year 1 (Knickmeyer et al. 2008). It calibrates reaching, sitting, pursuit and the VOR. | A born granule expansion with Purkinje weights learned by LMS every ~10 ms. Its climbing-fibre error is the servo law's own corrective torque: feedback-error learning (Marr 1969; Albus 1971; Kawato and Gomi 1992). |
| thalamus | **p**. The event lines are the amygdala's "low road" (SD:1024). Every channel is summed ungated (R4, SD:1133). | Attention disengages better from about 3–4 months (Johnson 1990). | A per-channel gain from the stream, as the TRN gates (Wimmer et al. 2015). That is 9 scalars. |
| superior colliculus | **p**. A face template and the sound's side (SD:358). Visual onset orienting is refused as "a saliency map" (SD:1879). | Newborns orient to peripheral visual stimuli through the subcortical route (Bronson 1974; Johnson 1990). | An event line for onsets in the periphery, with the same born bias and the amygdala's gain. |
| brainstem | **p**. The VOR with its quick phase (`wt_world/body/sim/world.py:274`) and a breath reservoir (SD:718). The born cry is refused (SD:369). | Cries at birth; coos at about 2 months; canonical babble at 6–10 months (Oller 2000). | A respiratory rhythm on the lungs articulator, plus a PAG cry on pain or a low charge. Both can be overridden (Jürgens 2002). |
| spinal cord | **p**. Servo tone, withdrawal and grasp (SD:226–240, 356–357; `wt_world/body/sim/reflexes.py`). The CPG is refused (SD:362). | Alternating kicks and general movements (Thelen 1979; Prechtl 1990). Two stepping primitives present at birth (Dominici et al. 2011). | A half-centre oscillator per limb on W1's flexion vectors (SD:1258), with the legs alternating and the amplitude scaled by the gate (Brown 1911). |
| hypothalamus | **p**. The charge drive (SD:962); sleep pressure +1 a tick (AR:596); no temperature, no circadian clock. | A circadian rhythm emerges by about 2–4 months (Rivkees 2003). | Motor heat as a drive, since the real G1 reports each motor's temperature (unitree_hg `MotorState`). Sleep by processes S and C (Borbély 1982), with C set by the light channel (SD:943). |
| neuromodulators | **p**. Dopamine as TD error (AR:403–478); mood, its tonic level, sets every readout's sharpness (`wt_sim/body/core/mouth.py:339,611`). No NE, ACh or 5-HT. | All present. | Doya's map (2002): NE sets the temperature from the running forecast error (Aston-Jones and Cohen 2005); ACh sets the gain and plasticity per channel (Yu and Dayan 2005); 5-HT sets the horizon (Miyazaki et al. 2014). |
| sleep stages | **p**. NREM replay, then REM imagination (AR:334–401; R8, SD:1149). The world is paused (SD:945; `world.py:503`). One pass, no cycles. | Active sleep is about half of a newborn's sleep (Roffwarg, Muzio and Dement 1966). About 10 twitches a minute (Sokoloff et al. 2020). | REM in a live, dark world, with brainstem twitches whose reafference trains `act_inv` and the forward half (Blumberg, Marques and Iida 2013). |
| development | **M**. The design never mentions critical periods or myelination. | Plasticity windows (Hensch 2005); experience-expectant maturation (Greenough, Black and Wallace 1987). | A maturation schedule born by age: acuity, and per-channel plasticity windows. |
| joint attention | **p**. The parent follows the child's fovea (SD:851). | Prefers direct gaze at birth (Farroni et al. 2002); follows gaze at 6–12 months (Butterworth and Jarrett 1991). | Nothing new in the body. |
| imitation/mirror | **p**. The voice imitates through `act_inv` reading heard sound (SD:758–760), and 2 of 12 of its words are accepted (SD:767). No mirror module (SD:1687). | Imitates vowels at 12–20 weeks (Kuhl and Meltzoff 1996). The mirror mapping is learned, and being imitated trains it (Heyes 2010). | Auditory-target feedback control as a control error, not a reward (Guenther 2006). The parent mirrors the child's gestures; her method is ours (SD:131). |
| language | **p**. The token scaffold in and out (SD:800–822). A word from the tract is "not forecast" (SD:1371). | Understands common nouns at 6–9 months (Bergelson and Swingley 2012). | The above, and keep the scaffold-removal tests. |

## The gaps ranked by what they block

1. **Cerebellum → moving and reaching.** A limp trunk falls with a time constant of about 0.19 s, about one tick (SD:1398), so a loop acting once a tick cannot hold a sit. Feedback-error learning is learned, not a controller; the servo law is its innate teacher. Cost: about 1–2 ms a tick (my estimate, not measured).
2. **Spinal CPG → rolling (M4), and the babble that `act_inv` learns from.** The design refused the stepping generator because kicking is the same pattern, "which babble already gives" (SD:362). But the born loop's units average 1.4 ticks, close to the fresh draws that gave 0 rolls (SD:336). The refusal's premise has failed. A kicking oscillator is not a balance or posture law, but reversing the refusal is a ruling for the owner to confirm.
3. **REM twitches in a live world → reaching.** `act_inv`'s reliability gates every guide (SD:344), and twitches give it clean single-joint pairs of act and reafference. The physics runs at 10.9–16.9× real time (SD:401–402), the eyes dark. This adds to REM.
4. **SC visual onset and a V1 front end → looking (M1), the never-shown toy (M2).** A sound onset turns the child; a visual one does not (SD:358 against 1879). The fovea's code is below a newborn's acuity.
5. **Breath rhythm and cry → babbling.** Only 6–7% of born-babble ticks sound (SD:742); the newborn's first vocal loop is missing.
6. **Auditory targets → a first word from the tract (M6t).** The measured route accepts 2 of 12 words (SD:767).
7. **Hippocampal recall into action, and heading → understanding.** This covers object permanence, deferred imitation, and where the toy went.
8. **Thalamic/ACh gain, and NE/5-HT → understanding and credit.** It binds the word to the fovea's object. 5-HT's horizon bears on risk 1's delay (SD:1397).
9. **Development schedule → phonetic categories and acuity.**
10. **Temperature and circadian sleep → the robot's transfer.** No milestone in year 1 needs them.

**Adequate as designed:** the amygdala, dopamine and the critics, the gates per limb, the VOR, withdrawal and grasp, the cochlea, pain and charge.
