# Code review of body/life.py and the parent: findings, the robot gap and the refactor plan

Nothing in the project was changed. "Constant" below means a new switch that is off by default, so the determinism digest stays the same. It gets measured on a copy before the served body uses it.

## 1. Confirmed defects, ranked by harm to the body's learning

### Medium: affecting the served body now

1. **Tired memories never recover (body/life.py:602).** `_tire` points `store.A` at a new tensor. Every write of a new slot points it back at FastStore's buffer, and the buffer only holds tiring from write ticks, never the recovery. Since 09-19 the most-used memories have been pushed down all day, some close to zero (about -13.8 logits). This bites while the parent types, which is just before the child replies. On a tiny body, the best match's availability averaged 0.62 against 0.71 with the fix.
   - Fix: `self.store.A.copy_(1 - rc*(1 - self.store.A))`. This gives exactly the behaviour of the Store used before 09-19.
   - Needs a constant, plus a test that writes a new slot after tiring (test 44 never does).
2. **Some smiles are never felt (body/caregiver.py:331).** When the face returns to 0 and the next smile is sent in the same poll, the two posts land milliseconds apart with no tick between them, so the body feels nothing. About 12% of known-word smiles are lost, and so is the first step of about a quarter of answer smiles. Every miss logged as "late" is really a refractory drop.
   - Fix now, log only: relabel those rows.
   - Fix as env constant FACE_REST_SEEN: wait for one tick at face 0 before the next smile.
3. **Long lines are silently dropped (body/teacher.py:279).** `clean()` throws away lines over 40 characters or with an apostrophe. The four POOL questions in night_cycle.sh have never been typed, so their answers arrive with no question and the answer turn is lost. In the last 2500 queue rows, 553 of 10000 lines were dropped.
   - Fix, in the scripts that feed the queue only: shorten the four questions, remove the apostrophe from the allowed set in ops/read_stories.py, and make ops/queue_depth.py count lines through `clean()`.

### Low: affecting the served body now

4. **A rest is logged as the gate saying no (life.py:1398-1407).** A word that ends in a rest is stored as (acted 0, p 1). With the save's gate_vigor 0, that gives an eligibility of -1 on a tick the gate never decided. A "go" followed by a sampled rest is stored as (0, p). It is rare now (2 such words in 15 days), but under chunk_gate 1 it would happen at every rest inside a word.
   - Fix before adopting chunk_gate: record the gate's own draw and use it for eligibility. Needs a constant.
5. **The actor's trace decays per word, not per tick (life.py:1436).** Dopamine multiplies the trace every tick, so credit smears across about 16 words and through silences. Behaviour barely changes (the actor's vote is under 0.09 logit), but nothing measured about the actor or actor_lr can be trusted until this is fixed. Constant `actor_trace_tick`.
6. **Smiles never reach the felt entry (life.py:483).** `utt_entry felt` only sees the dopamine from the tick before each heard symbol. The typist never types within one tick of a smile, so smiles tag nothing. B1 therefore tested replay tagged by surprise, not by reward.
   - Constant `utt_entry_trace` (a tag lasting 32-64 ticks).
   - Correct the comments at lines 108 and 704.
7. **The felt mean starts cold and is not saved (life.py:708-711).** In B1 the opening greeting set the mean at 0.37 of typical. The first four facts entered 1.1 to 1.5 times too strong. That is worth about +0.26 dreamt facts, which does not overturn "4 of 10".
   - Save and restore the mean (no constant needed).
   - Warm-up behind `utt_entry_warm`.
8. **The gate's credit counts dopamine from before the act (life.py:1558).** Not biased, but about 21% more noise. Constant `elig_from`.
9. **night_ticks lands on the wrong step (life.py:996 and 2074).** The discount hits the step from the first to the second morning tick. No transition across the night has ever been learned. The cost is one bad sample a day. Either drop `--night-ticks` at a boundary, or add constant `night_bridge` to make it work as the spec says.
10. **A working-memory latch breaks the fast critic's evidence chain (life.py:1112).** It touches about 1% of transitions. Dopamine itself is correct, so the obvious fix would be wrong. Constant `wm_chain`.
11. **offset_foresee trains on its own guess (life.py:947).** When p(rest) crosses the threshold, the cortex's guess becomes an end mark that it then learns from. It also opens the floor in the middle of one-handed parent lines. Constant `foresee_retract`.
12. **The end of a line depends on mood (life.py:946).** That p(rest) is read at a sharpness mood sets between 20 and 50. Constant `foresee_sharp`, then re-measure the 0.2 threshold.
13. **The store reads and tires twice a tick (life.py:1458).** Correct the documentation. Add `read_tire_once` only if the documented rate is wanted.
14. **Every night draws the maximum dreams (life.py:497).** The write counter includes merges, so every night draws 2048 dreams. Constant `night_count='slots'`.
15. **Faded keys are stored malformed (model.py:884).** Keys fainter than 1e-12 (after 930 to 1650 quiet ticks) are stored as short vectors that can never be recalled. This has been rare since 09-19. Constant `store_key_rescale`.
16. **A failed save causes an extra night (life.py:2091).** It schedules another night 12000 ticks later, so both memories fade twice a day while the disk stays full. It has never fired, but the disk is 94% full. Fix: give `save()` its own try block. No constant needed.
17. **The line popped at nightfall is lost (teacher.py:215).** This happened on 5 of the last 10 nights. Env constant UNSAID_BACK.
18. **"hot." gets no smile (caregiver.py:321).** It also drains attention. That is about 2% of smiles, and the bias is against the end mark. Add KNOWN_MARK, stripping only a trailing mark.
19. **The answer smile pays for echoing the question (caregiver.py:316).** About a third of answer smiles go to a word from the question, though usually as the start of a right answer. Add ANSWER_ASKED: the expected words are the answer's words minus the question's, keeping both "or" choices and accepting fused words. The fix proposed with the finding would leave nothing to reward.
20. **Negligible:**
    - life.py:2073: the morning continues the evening's word (`night_ends_word`).
    - life.py:2472: a save taken mid-line loses that line (`load_ends_line`).
    - life.py:428: the optimizers are not saved; no change needed.

## 3. What stands between this body and a robot

These block a robot. None of them blocks the diary demo:
- One symbol per tick, one world channel, one mouth, one readout.
- The world going quiet is both the event clock and the turn signal, so a stream that never goes quiet never ends an event.
- The night replays only the world's typed lines, never the body's own acts.
- The actor's unit is a word ended by a space, and one gate serves the whole body.
- The symbol table is frozen and random, and some organs grow with the vocabulary.
- The loop runs at 0.15 s with no deadline and no cached cortex while awake, and inputs wait in a queue.
- Sleep comes by tick count, inside a tick.
- Reward is felt only on face-level changes. A jittery face reader produced 77 false smiles in 300 ticks.
- One store write per symbol, and the tokenizer stands in for the anatomy.

These fall into five areas of work:
- an Anatomy object in place of the tokenizer;
- a frame of several channels per tick, with a gate per effector;
- event boundaries from prediction error, plus a separate channel for whose turn it is;
- motor programs that learn when to stop;
- a loop with a deadline, safe sleep and a steadier reward signal.

Already general: the tick's phases, the value ladder, dopamine from the fast critic, the ventral critic, the gate's three-factor lesson and tag, the body's copy of its own acts, the cortex, the store and its cortex key, the night, feelings, the face organ, the settle law, deliberation when unsure, chunk_gate, and save/load with organ migration.

## 4. Refactor plan, and why it waits for the demo

**The check does not cover the served body.** tools/determinism_check.py runs on flags alone, but 26 served constants live only in the save. So the check never runs the served dopamine, ventral critic, actor, word-level mouth, ear, save/load or sleep switch, and its hash leaves out the bands, the face organ and rbar. The 80 tests have the same gap. Until that is fixed, a refactor cannot be shown to change nothing on the body used in the demo.

**What to remove:**
- 67 of 204 constants have no effect on the served body. For example, vcrit_w 0.3 is set in the flags and does nothing.
- Retired mechanisms kept as instruments: store dreams, forecast REM, cortex key, episode_chain, softmax chooser, own_store, reward_gain, SGD gate, the gradient ventral critics, the unbatched night.
- Store marks written every tick that nothing reads, and four stale keys in the save.
- In total, about 400-450 lines of life.py.

**What to merge:** the reliability estimator (3 copies), the least-squares solvers (4), the stream rollouts, the rest masks (11), and the save/load field lists that are kept by hand (the source of the 9th and 13th defects).

**How to split life.py:** as mixins first, so state and call order stay the same: physiology, senses, memory, cortex, mouth (with a Reflexes object for item 50), critics, actor, night, persistence and instruments.

**Order of work:**
1. Fix the check: a served profile, a chunk_gate/felt profile, a hash of the full state, and a save/load round trip. About half a day.
2. The mechanical split, with digests unchanged. About a day.
3. Tidy, change the defaults to the served values, then delete dead paths one per commit. One to two days.

Watch three things along the way: the order of random draws, the order organs are built in, and the floating-point order in the least-squares updates.

**My advice:** no full refactor before the demo. Before it, only fixes 1-3 are worth doing, as constants measured on a copy, plus the separate try block for the save in item 16.
